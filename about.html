About Wardrobe Edit

Wardrobe Edit is a small, independently run fashion site. We put together curated lists of clothing, footwear, and accessories that we think are worth your money — from everyday basics to the occasional statement piece.

We don't manufacture or sell anything ourselves. Every product featured on this site links out to retailers where you can view full details and complete your purchase, most often Amazon.

Our picks are based on research, trends, and genuine opinion. As with any recommendation site, we may earn a small commission when you buy through our links — see our Affiliate Disclaimer for details. This never affects the price you pay.

Have a question, a product suggestion, or feedback on the site? Reach out through our Contact page — we read everything.
